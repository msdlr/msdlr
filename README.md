## About me

I'm got my PhD in Computer Science at **Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha (UCLM), Spain**, and a member of the **High Performance Architecture and Networks (RAAP)** research group.

My research has focused on **High-Performance Computing (HPC) interconnection networks**, particularly **Quality of Service (QoS)**, **power-saving mechanisms**, traffic modeling, and network simulation for the **BXIv3** architecture. I have several years of experience developing and extending the OmNet++-based **SAURON** network simulator and participated in the European **RED-SEA** project for the core topics of my thesis.

Outside academia I enjoy building tools, automating everything I can, and contributing to open-source software.
